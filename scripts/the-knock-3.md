# The Knock (Part 3 - Final)

Rule: In this town, when the streetlights go out, you never open the door.
Music: Numbers (low) - spooky
Voice: user supplies the narration (eleven_v4, same voice as Parts 1-2, prompt below). Do NOT generate voice unless the user says so.

Follows the viewers' write-in pick from Part 2 (the "hidden option"): the streetlights are back on, so go outside.
Twist ending: callback to Part 1 ("Dad never calls me kiddo" / "Something followed us") - the Knocker came home with Dad.

## Narration

You picked the option I didn't even think of. The streetlights were back on. Knockers only come in the dark.

So I ran.

Down the hall. Past the stairs. I didn't look up. I threw open the front door, and the morning hit my face.

Mrs. Parker was getting her newspaper. She saw my face and didn't ask a single question. She just pulled me inside.

Mom was still on the phone. "Stay there, bug. We're five minutes away."

From the Parkers' window, I could see my house. My bedroom curtain moved.

Something was standing in my window. Wearing my hoodie. It lifted its hand and waved. Slowly. The same way I wave.

Then Mom and Dad's car pulled into the driveway. I ran across the street faster than I've ever run.

Mom hugged me so hard my feet left the ground. "Bug, you're okay. You're okay."

Dad knelt down and messed up my hair. He smiled at me.

"Hey, kiddo."

I looked at Mom. She didn't notice.

Last night, something knocked on our back door and said, "Something followed us."

It wasn't lying.

Dad never calls me kiddo.

# Completely Normal

Rule: In this town, everything is completely normal.
Music: Numbers
Voice: ElevenLabs Titan (dtSEyYGNJqjrtBArPCVZ), eleven_v4, emotion tags below, voice_fx +5.5 st

Format: a run of build-ups that end in something ordinary (night time, the mailman, a branch, the garbage truck,
a popsicle) - then the one time it might be real, he sleeps through it.

## Narration

In this town, everything is completely normal. It rains. It gets sunny. Sometimes, it gets cold.

Until one day, the sky turned dark. The birds went quiet. The streetlights flickered on, one by one. Everyone looked up. It was just night time.

Then, I heard footsteps behind me. Slow. Heavy. Getting closer. I turned around. It was the mailman. He had a package for my mom.

That night, something tapped on my window. Tap. Tap. Tap. I hid under my blanket. I counted to three. I ripped the curtain open. It was a branch.

The next morning, the ground started shaking. The windows rattled. The cups slid off the table. It was the garbage truck. It comes every Tuesday.

Then, my little sister disappeared. Her bed was empty. Her shoes were gone. The front door was wide open. She was in the backyard, eating a popsicle.

By now, I'd stopped expecting anything. So when the sky turned purple, and the ground started glowing, and a voice said my name, I just went back to sleep.

In this town, everything is completely normal. Probably.

# Nothing Happens Here

Rule: In this town, everything is normal.
Music: Numbers
Voice: ElevenLabs Titan (dtSEyYGNJqjrtBArPCVZ), eleven_v4, emotion tags below, voice_fx +5.5 st

## Narration

In this town, everything is normal. The haunted mansion is just a dentist's office. The cursed well is full of pennies. The fortune teller only predicts rain, and she's usually right.

Then, the tapping started. Tap. Tap. Tap. Every night at three a.m., on my bedroom window.

On the second night, I heard footsteps in the hall. On the third night, the fridge was glowing in the dark. And then the vent started whispering. "Over. Do you copy? Over."

I stopped sleeping. I drew maps of every sound. My cousin said our house was built on an old graveyard. My cousin also thinks pigeons are robots.

On the seventh night, the tapping got louder. I grabbed my flashlight. I counted to three. And I ripped the curtain open.

It was a branch. Just a branch, scratching the glass in the wind.

The footsteps were my little sister, sneaking cookies. The glowing fridge was my little sister, sneaking milk. The whispers were my cousin's walkie-talkie, stuck in the vent since summer.

The next day, I trimmed the tree. That night, I slept for eleven hours.

In this town, everything is normal. And honestly? I like it that way.

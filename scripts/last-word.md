# The Last Word

Rule: In this town, every dog can say one word a year.
Music: Numbers
Voice: user supplies the narration (Titan, eleven_v4, prompt below). Do NOT generate voice unless the user says so.

Tone: funny worldbuilding -> warm boy-and-dog years -> the dog stops using his words -> gentle, kid-safe goodbye
(old age, asleep, never shown as scary) -> the saved-up words pay off.

## Narration

In this town, every dog can say one word a year. Most dogs waste it. My neighbor's poodle said "Bacon." Every year. The mailman's dog said "No."

I got Biscuit on my fifth birthday. He was a puppy with one floppy ear. His first word was "Ball." His second was "Ball" again. I laughed so hard I fell off the couch.

Then, when I was eight, Biscuit stopped talking. Every year, on his birthday, I waited. Nothing. Just his tail, thumping on the floor.

Mom said maybe he was saving them. I said dogs don't save words. Dogs say "Ball."

We grew up together. He walked me to school. He slept on my feet. He got slower. Grayer. His floppy ear got floppier.

This spring, the vet said he was very old. That it would be soon. That I should stay close.

So I stayed close. The last night, I lay on the floor next to him. His breathing was slow.

Then he lifted his head. And he used every word he'd saved.

"Thank you for picking me. I would pick you too."

Then he put his head on my hand. And he went to sleep.

I still say goodnight to his bed. Some nights, I swear I hear his tail.

# The Third Mark

Rule: In this town, everyone is born with a mark on their wrist.

## Narration

In this town, everyone is born with a mark on their wrist. A leaf means you're prey. A claw means you're a predator.

Predators get the front of the lunch line. Prey get the seats by the trash cans. My cousin got a claw and hasn't carried his own backpack since. And there's a third mark nobody's seen in a hundred years. Alpha.

I had a claw. At least, that's what the marker said. Every morning, I drew it on. Every night, I washed it off and stared at my leaf.

Then, it rained at recess. Kai, the biggest predator in school, grabbed my wrist. The claw ran down my arm in black streaks. "He's prey," Kai said. "He's been faking it."

Everyone circled me. "Show us," they said. "Show us what you really are." My wrist started to burn. The leaf glowed. Then it cracked open like an egg. And underneath, there was a crown.

Kai let go. Everyone stepped back. "Alpha," someone whispered. Kai bowed his head. "What do we do now?" I looked at the kids by the trash cans. Prey kids. Like me. "Now," I said, "nobody eats by the trash cans."

# The Monster in My Closet

Rule: In this town, every closet has a monster in it.
Music: Numbers
Voice: ElevenLabs Titan (dtSEyYGNJqjrtBArPCVZ), eleven_v4, emotion tags below, voice_fx +5.5 st

Twist: he is his own closet monster (time loop). He stays hidden because if young-him had a friend he'd never open the
door at midnight - so he lets himself be lonely and only hums. Parents are busy (workaholic dad, mom never home).

## Narration

In this town, every closet has a monster in it. They're not scary. They're roommates. Some steal socks. Some snore so loud the hangers rattle. My cousin's monster does his homework. Badly.

You get your monster the day you're born. It moves in, and it never leaves.

But my closet was empty.

Dad worked late. Every night. Mom worked even later. Dinner was a sticky note on the fridge. "Love you. Microwave for two minutes."

So I really wanted a monster. Just someone to talk to. I left cookies. I left the nightlight on. I even made a sign: "Monster Wanted." Every night, I opened the closet. Nothing.

The cookies were always gone by morning, though. And Mom said when I was a baby, I never cried at night. Something always hummed me back to sleep.

On my twelfth birthday, nobody came home. But taped inside my closet door, I found a note. Old. Yellow. In my handwriting. "Don't open the closet at midnight. Trust me."

I didn't write that. At least... not yet.

At midnight, I opened it anyway. I just didn't want to be alone.

It wasn't empty anymore. It was a tunnel, glowing at the bottom. I fell, and fell, and landed... in a closet. My closet. Twelve years ago. Through the crack in the door, there was a crib. And a baby. Crying. Me.

My closet was never empty. Its monster was just hiding.

I could have knocked. Every single night, I could have knocked. But if he'd had a friend, he never would've opened that door at midnight. And I'd never be here to hum when he cries.

So I stayed in the dark. I ate the cookies. I read the sticky notes too.

Tonight, he turns twelve. Nobody's coming home. I'm taping a note inside the door. In my handwriting. "Don't open the closet at midnight. Trust me."

He won't listen. I didn't.

# The Trade (Part 1)

Rule: In this town, the pet shop on Elm Street can bring any pet back to life. But it costs something.
Music: Numbers
Voice: user supplies the narration (Titan, eleven_v4, prompt below). Do NOT generate voice unless the user says so.

Format: sad cliffhanger. Part 1 ends on a choice; viewers vote A or B in the comments and Part 2 follows the winning choice.
Kid-safe: the dog dies of old age, peacefully, off screen.

## Narration

In this town, there's a pet shop on Elm Street that can bring any pet back. My neighbor's goldfish has been back four times. Mrs. Patel's cat came back so often it has a punch card.

But it costs something. Something different for everyone.

Biscuit was my dog for eleven years. He had one floppy ear and he snored like a lawnmower. He walked me to school every single day, even in the rain.

Last Tuesday, he didn't wake up. He was just old. The vet said he went in his sleep.

So I went to Elm Street.

The shop was dark and smelled like cinnamon. The old man behind the counter didn't even look up. "Biscuit," he said. "The one with the floppy ear."

I hadn't told him that.

He slid two cards across the counter.

The first card said: "He comes back. But he won't remember you. Not one day."

The second card said: "He stays gone. But you'll remember every single day. Forever. Even the last one."

The old man finally looked at me. "You get one choice. You can't come back."

My hand is shaking over the cards.

A, or B? Which one do I pick?
